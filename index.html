About the teacher

With over twenty years in the classroom, Ms. Nausheen Sohail has guided hundreds of students through Cambridge O Level Urdu B and A Level Urdu, building both strong grades and genuine confidence in the language.

Her lessons focus on what actually matters for the exam: essay structure, comprehension technique, and precise vocabulary, taught in a calm, patient style that has made her one of the most sought-after private tutors in her subject.

This platform brings her classroom online — announcements, recorded lectures, and live class links, all in one place for enrolled students.
