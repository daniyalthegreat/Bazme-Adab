Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Bazme Adab ("we", "us", "the site") is a private Urdu tuition platform run by Ms. Nausheen Sohail. This policy explains what information we collect from students who use this site, why we collect it, and how it's protected.

1. Information we collect

When you register an account, we collect your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from your Google account instead of asking you to type them in.

If you submit a payment, we collect the screenshot you upload as proof of payment, along with any note you add (such as a transaction reference). We do not collect or store your bank account numbers, card numbers, or any banking credentials — you send payment directly through your own banking app, outside this site.

We also store the tasks you add to your personal to-do list, and a record of which announcements have been posted (these are visible to all enrolled students, not personal data about you specifically).

2. How we use this information

Your name and email are used to identify your account, let you log in, and allow Ms. Nausheen to contact you regarding your enrolment. Payment screenshots are used solely to verify your payment and grant access to your plan — they are reviewed by Ms. Nausheen and are not shared with anyone else.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies

This site uses one type of cookie: a login session cookie, set by our authentication provider (Supabase), which keeps you signed in between visits. This cookie is strictly necessary for the site to function and does not track you across other websites. We do not currently use advertising or marketing cookies.

4. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to run this site:

  • Supabase — stores your account data and payment receipt files securely, and handles login (including "Sign in with Google")
  • Google — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google's own privacy policy also applies to that sign-in process
  • Vercel — hosts this website

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices, which you can review on their respective websites.

5. Data security

Your account data is protected by database-level security rules that ensure only you can see your own to-do list and your own payment history — not even other students can see this information. Only Ms. Nausheen, as the site administrator, can view submitted payment receipts and the list of registered students, for the purpose of managing enrolment.

6. Data retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account remains active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact Ms. Nausheen directly using the details on our About section.

7. Children's privacy

This site is intended for O Level and A Level students, who may be minors. We only collect the minimum information needed to provide tuition (name, email, and payment confirmation) and do not knowingly collect any other personal information about minors.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Ms. Nausheen Sohail through the contact details provided on the site.
